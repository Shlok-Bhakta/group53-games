# Golf

AGENTS.md is the overview and the `framework` skill covers matches, turns, pushes and the harness; the `ui` skill covers `FullscreenStage`, `FloatingTopBar` and landscape. This is what you need on top of them to change golf.

The rule behind every mode: nothing may favor whoever has the most free time (the caddie and the live hole clock exist so sitting out never pays). Still to come: an Async mode where each hole is played in one sitting and items picked up on hole N land on everyone else's hole N+1, and Race.

## Modes and settings

Two modes, `game: "golf"`, in `convex/golf/modes.ts` (`GOLF_GAME`, `GOLF_MODES`), 2 to 8 players, so the host taps Start (or it starts when all 8 seats fill):

| Mode | Kind | Default holes | Own setting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `golf:turns` "Turn-based" | async | 6 (`HOLES_FOR_A_DAY_OF_TURNS`) | Time to shoot (`shotTime`, minutes: No limit by default, 1h, 4h, 8h, 1 day) |
| `golf:live` "Live" | realtime | 9 (`HOLES_LIVE`) | Hole clock (`holeTime`: 2, 3 or 5 min) |

Shared settings (`golf(holes)`): Course (0 Generated, 1 Bumper Plains, 2 Cold Start), Holes (3/6/9/18; a hand-made course has nine, eighteen of one is both courses, `holeCount`/`courseHole` in `build.ts`), and only for a generated course (`when: GENERATED_COURSE`) Terrain (Grass, Ice, Both), Holes like (Mini golf, Fairways, Both), Hazards (Few, Some, Lots) and Seed (`kind: "seed"`: 0 rolls one when the game's made, `withSeeds` in `matches.create`; a rematch rolls a new one; never remembered on the phone). Then Item boxes (None, Few keeps every other box, Normal, Lots adds as many again in clear ground, `boxesFor`), Items (`kind: "mask"`, a bit per `POWERS` entry, `powersOf`; `(1 << POWERS.length) - 1` is everything, and an all-on or all-off mask means "every item"), Ball hits (`collide`) and Safe near the cup (`mark`, only with ball hits on).

Endings: `holes` ("Last hole played", won "with the fewest strokes") plus the shared resign/timeout/last_player.

## Determinism (never break this)

Every phone and the server run the same world and must agree to the last bit; a bumper turns one bit into a different shot.
- `+ - * /` and `Math.sqrt` are exact IEEE 754 everywhere; `Math.sin/cos/tan/atan2/hypot/exp/log/pow`, `**` and friends are only "close", and JavaScriptCore (iPhones, Bun) and V8 (Android, the server, Node) disagree in the last bit. Lint bans them in `convex/golf/**` (not tests, not `dmath.ts`), plus `Math.random`, `Date.now`, `performance.now`.
- `dmath.ts`: `hyp`, `dsin`/`dcos` (Taylor to x^17 on [-π/4, π/4] after an octant reduction with a two-part π/2), `datan` (series on [0, tan(π/12)], widened with the π/6 identity and π/2 - atan(1/t)), `datan2`, `dacos` (`datan2(√(1-x²), x)`), `dexp`/`dlog` (2^k·e^r; log by halving into [√½, √2) then 2·atanh), `dpow`, `DPI`, `DTWO_PI`. About 1e-15 accurate.
- Course geometry counts as physics: hand-made holes, shapes and the generator use dmath too (a seed must build the same holes wherever it's built). `plains.ts` (the Crown's lollipop board and bumper ring) moved from Math.cos/sin/acos to dmath; that shifted those points by an ulp, and every solution still replays.
- Randomness is `rng(seed)` (mulberry32, `engine.ts`); a ball's draws use its player's id as a number.
- Statuses are absolute times on the ball's own clock (`frozenUntil`, `stunUntil`, `blindUntil`...), never countdowns, so the quiet jump ends them exactly as stepping would.
- `fingerprint(s)` hashes every bit of a world (each double's bytes). `world.test.ts` pins one scripted three-player Driftwood Bay game (`PINNED`): if it changes, the physics changed, every phone must update together and old games replay differently. Update it only on purpose. `determinism.test.ts` plays `scenarios.ts`'s chaotic eight-player games (live and turn-based, bags full of the items that throw balls about) under Node and, when Bun is installed (`~/.bun/bin/bun`, or `BUN=`), under Bun via `scripts/golf-fingerprints.ts`, and requires identical fingerprints. Without Bun the cross-engine test is skipped and says so in its name.
- Drawing code (`toy.ts`, the loop's camera and effects) may use `Math.*`: nothing there feeds back into the world.

## The engine (`convex/golf/engine.ts`)

- Top-down, y down (north is up the screen). Units: ball radius 0.38 (`BALL_R`), cup 0.72 (`CUP_R`), `MAX_SPEED` 32, `GRAVITY` 30, 240 steps a second (`DT`, `TPS` in world.ts: one tick is one step). `MAX_STROKES` 10 on your own; the open world has no limit.
- Ground (`Ground`, `DECEL` rolling resistance): green is fast mini-golf carpet; ice barely slows; sand fast; `packed` (Cold Start's fairways) about half grass; hazards (`HAZARDS`: water, lava, void, coolant, oob) end the shot: a stroke and back where it was hit from. `oob` is outside the walls, reachable only in the air. Greens are even-odd: a loop inside another is a hole (an island). Zones either set the ground (later zones win) or push (slopes with `dir` downhill, hills > 0 and bowls < 0, steepest halfway out; wind: a nudge on the ground, a real push in the air; boosts; conveyors give the ground its own velocity, and resistance acts on speed relative to it). `groundUnder` adds what players put down: ice over water (`patches`), honey over anything.
- `compile(course)` buckets walls into a coarse grid of cells (each wall in every cell within reach of a ball touching it), so `nearSegs` only checks nearby walls; a map in the void or the sea has no edge wall, so a ball flies off and drops in. `worldOf` caches compiled worlds by id, but only reuses one for the very same hole (a game's holes are new objects on every read; a reused id must never stand in for different walls), and clears past 120.
- `stepBall`: frozen balls stop; moving obstacles (`movers`: a spinning bar, a sliding crate, `poseOf` by time) push even a still ball; wells bend what flies; ramps launch (climbing costs speed); walls reflect the normal part and keep most of the tangent (Bouncy fires it back harder; nothing exceeds `MAX_SPEED * 2`); a Jumbo ball flattens bumpers; mines and boxes only touch a ball on the ground (`TOUCHING` height); portals both ways, not straight back; the cup takes a slow ball and lips out a fast one (rattles round the rim), nearest cup on a map with several. Rewinding (Mulligan) leaves opened boxes opened, so it can't farm them.
- `preview()` is the aim line: out to the first wall, then a short bounce. `canShoot`: still (or rolling on Coffee), not frozen, not stunned.

## Items and statuses

25 in `POWERS`, three in the bag (`BAG_SIZE`). `SOLO_POWERS` is what a box gives on your own; `POWERS` adds the ones that only touch other players (missile, horn, pistol, elephant, shield, freeze, flash, storm, honey). `INSTANT` happen when tapped, `TARGETED` need a spot or direction (`fireTargeted`), the rest arm the next shot (`armed`, then `shot` while it rolls). Ours: Ghost, Magnet, Brake (stop dead once), Mulligan, Big cup, Jump (one hop clearing walls and water), Bouncy. Super Battle Golf's: Rocket Driver (`maxSpeed` ×1.8; drawn as a burning ring while armed, a bang, a flame and orange streak), Spring Boots, Golf Cart (no slowing for a while), Jumbo Burger (`JUMBO` 2.2× radius), Smoke Bomb, Orbital Laser (lands after a moment, takes out bumpers and mines, throws balls), Landmine, Rail Gun (a line clearing bumpers and mines and throwing balls near it), Rocket Launcher (homes on the nearest player, `MISSILE`; an Electromagnet pops it), Air Horn (everyone within `REACH` overshoots their next putt), Flash Camera (no aim line, `BLIND_S`), Dueling Pistol (first ball on the line knocked and stunned, `PISTOL`), Elephant Gun (harder, kicks you back, `ELEPHANT`), Coffee (putt again while rolling, `COFFEE_S`), Electromagnet (`SHIELD_S`, items don't touch you), Freeze Bomb (ice cubes for `FREEZE_S`, water under it ice for `ICE_S`, radius `FREEZE_R`), Thunderstorm (`STORM`, bolts drawn from the world's rng), and Golf With Your Friends' Honey (`HONEY_S`). Anything reaching other players is worked out in `world.ts` (`applyCmd`; missiles and storms in `stepWorld`). A knocked ball is `knocked` (with `knockFree`, a knock into a hazard goes back without a penalty). The phone's names and icons are `items.ts`; the server's names `names.ts`.

## The world and the netcode (`world.ts`, `convex/golf.ts`, `useGolfNet.ts`, `useGolfLoop.ts`)

- A world is every ball on one course sharing boxes, bumpers, mines and lasers (the same arrays, changed in place), knocking into each other in join order when `collide` (push apart, trade speed along the line, bigger balls give less; a still ball is knocked into a roll; dead on top, apart along x).
- Everything is a command at a tick (`Cmd`: join, leave, shot, use, fire, hop, brake, reset, turn, pickup; `bag` on a join is the carry). `cmdOrder` (tick, then `by`, then `seq`) is the one order every phone agrees on. `advance(s, w, to, cmds)` runs to `to`, applying each command at its tick and skipping any before the world's tick.
- **A move is one insert.** `golf:act` inserts into `golfCommands`, stamped with the tick the player saw (`clientTick`), honoured within `[server - MAX_LAG, server + SLACK]`; it never writes anything another player writes, so simultaneous players never conflict. Power is capped and junk refused. Waking a sleeping world is the only other write. A phone's `seq` starts from the clock (`Date.now() % 1e9`, set on its first send) so a reload never reuses one.
- `golf:tick` settles the snapshot (`golfSnapshots`) to `settledAt` reading only the settled range of commands (so it never conflicts with new ones), once a second while anything moves, rewriting the row only every `SNAPSHOT_EVERY` or when the world goes still, because every write goes to every phone. It sleeps when the world is `quiet` and nothing's happened since; reading past the settled range there can conflict with a landing command, and Convex just reruns one. A world that isn't quiet stays awake so its snapshot never falls far behind. Each run starts from the last written snapshot, so the sinks counted in a run that writes one are exactly the uncounted ones: `golfScores` counts each sink once, from the server's own run (unfakeable). `golf:here` restarts a game hole that says awake but hasn't settled in a while.
- **Quiet fast-forward**: a world where every ball sits still can't change until a command or a timer (box back in `BOX_BACK` 20 s, bumper `BUMPER_BACK` 25 s, map mine `MINE_BACK` 15 s, sink respawn `SINK_RESPAWN`), so `advance` jumps straight there, moving every ball's clock `t` to exactly what stepping would have (obstacles and statuses are drawn from it; without that they froze while still and jumped on the next shot). An idle day costs nothing. `quiet` ignores a moving obstacle unless it really sweeps a ball's spot.
- `pack`/`unpack`: the shared arrays once, not per ball, no events, through JSON (snapshots from before some fields existed lack them; `unpack` fills defaults).
- Phones: `golf:world` (world, snapshot, players; changes with the snapshot or a new player) and `golf:commands` from the snapshot's tick. **Pending commands**: your own command plays at once from `n.pending`, merged by `withPending(server, pending, baseTick)`, never a Convex optimistic update, because the commands query unloads for a whole round trip every time the snapshot moves (on airplane wifi a shot in that gap sat still over a second, then jumped or was back on the tee). A pending command drops once it's in the server's list or confirmed and inside the snapshot; unconfirmed and older than the snapshot, it plays from the snapshot on rather than vanishing. "Slow connection" shows in the dock while one's unconfirmed over `SLOW_MS`.
- Rebuilds: new commands or snapshot rebuild from the snapshot; every ball glides from where it was drawn to where it is (`EASE_S`, `EASE_FAR_S`), only sinking-and-back-to-a-tee jumps. Balls are drawn between ticks (`prev`). Events from a rebuild play only if recent and unheard (`seen`), except your own sink, which always shows its score however late the news.
- Join/leave: joining puts a ball on the first free tee (`tees`); joining with a ball already there is ignored. On a practice hole your ball goes when you leave (`golf:leave`, which runs the hole to now so an unsettled sink counts); a phone that vanished is cleared when someone next joins and it's been absent 30 s (`sweep`). The open world keeps your ball. A game's balls stay put whoever leaves.

## Rounds (`round.ts`, the game half of `convex/golf.ts`)

- `GolfState`: `total`, `holes` (built so far: name, par, theme, open), `hole` (-1 until hole 1 is built), `worldId`, `scores[hole][seat]` (null if resigned by then), `sunk` (false = picked up), `out` (resign order), `bags` (carry per seat), `turnTick` and `spots` (turn-based: where every ball sat when the turn started, to tell who got knocked and push them), `idle` (caddie turns in a row), `order` (honors), `waiting`, `builtAt`, `clockAt`/`nextAt` (live), `last` ("Chris sank it, 3", for lists and previews).
- Building: `golf:build` (an action) builds holes first to last into `golfCourses` (`addHole`), hole 1 first so the game starts while the rest are made, using a matching preview's hole if one exists (`genKey`); each hole's id is its world key `m:<match>:<n>` (`holeKey`) because compiled walls and distances are cached by id. `here` restarts a build that stopped.
- `startHole`: a world with every ball already on the tee holding its carry, rules `{ cap: par + 4 (capOf), turns, knockFree, markR: MARK_R }` (`done` when sunk or capped; marked balls by the cup let others roll through; items still hit), the opening state saved in `golfStarts`, then the first turn or the clock.
- **Turn order** (`nextUp`): anyone who hasn't teed off, in honors order (`honors`: best score on the last hole first, ties keeping the order they teed off; hole 1 in seat order, seats shuffled at the start), then farthest by walking distance (`distance.ts`, Dijkstra on a grid round rails, walls and water; islands fall back to straight line plus a toll), ties by honors. `giveTurn` sends a "turn" command (so every phone and the world enforce it: the world ignores anyone else's shot or items and a second shot, Coffee's putt aside; `act` refuses commands from before the turn started or on its very tick), a push and the clock, through `passTurn`. The turn ends once its ball is done, or has shot and everything's still (`roundTick`). `UNTIL_NEXT_TURN` statuses (Flash Camera until your shot, Electromagnet, ice and honey until the owner's next turn) wear off on the "turn" command.
- **The caddie**: with Time to shoot set, `golf:shotTimeout` calls `golf:caddie` (an action: working out the shot can take over a second) which reads the world (`caddieInfo`), takes the best of a fan (`bot.ts` `bestShot`: angles round the circle plus straight at the cup) with shaky hands, only goes for the cup within `CADDIE_SINK` (never an ace), and `caddieShoot` puts it in only if it's still their turn and the ball hasn't moved. If it can't, it retries later. Three in a row (`IDLE_OUT`) and the caddie takes all their turns straight off with no "Your shot" push, until they open the game (`golf:here`) or are looking at it. No limit (default): the turn waits, with the 8-hour reminders.
- **Live**: everyone shoots any time; `golf:holeClock` picks up whoever's left with par plus four (a "pickup" command; a ball already dropping isn't picked up), so sitting out never pays; `nextHole` starts the next after a moment to see the scores.
- `endHole` (from the settled world, once every ball's done): scores, sunk, bags, then the next hole or `finishBySeats(standings)` (fewest strokes, ties share; resigned after, first to go last). Resigning takes your ball off; you place last; if it was your turn it passes.
- **Catching up** (`GolfGame.tsx`, the replay in `useGolfLoop.ts`): each phone keeps `golf-seen:<match>` (hole and tick, written about once a second only while everything's still and not in the first moments after coming back). Opening or returning to a turn-based game plays every shot since from `golf:history` (the hole's start plus its commands): each shot in real time from a moment before it's hit (`LEAD`), the waits skipped, the camera on the shooter with their whole path drawn, hole by hole with each finished hole's `HoleCard`, then live. Skip jumps to now. A game that ended while you were away plays out before the result. Holes from before starts were kept return null and can't be played back.

## Generator and solver

- `gen.ts` (`generateHole(opts)`, server only, its output stored): mini boards from the hand-made vocabulary (straight, dogleg, Z, hairpin, ribbon, crown of bumpers, pinball table, islands joined by ramps, 45° zigzag) drawn into a signed field, traced by marching squares and simplified (RDP), then obstacles each placed with room (bumpers on the line, baffles from alternate sides, pads, sand by the cup, ponds with a way round, ice, slopes, mounds, a spinner, a crate, mines); or fairway holes (land in a lake or the void, rough, fairway and green mown on as zones, bunkers on the outside of the bend and by the green, a pond on the inside, a river with a bridge, wind). Every zone is cut to the board (the ground under a ball is the zone's first, so an overhang would be ground out in the void). `problems()` rejects nonsense (sand in water, bridges that don't land, bumper pockets that wedge a ball, boxes in hazards, no way round). Then `playHole` four times with shaky hands: it must sink it and not in one every time; par from its average; a slog (far more than a stroke per 9 units mini, per 60 fairway) is rejected. A failure retries from the next seed; everything counts attempts, never time, so a seed always builds the same holes. **Changing the generator changes what old seeds build.**
- `bun scripts/golf-gen.ts --seeds 1,2 --terrain 2 --style 2` draws holes to `e2e/.state/golf-gen`.
- Every hand-made hole must stay sinkable: `bun scripts/golf-solve.ts` finds a line round each (`scenarios.ts` `start`/`takeShot`) and plays it with shaky hands to judge par, writing `solutions.ts`, which `holes.test.ts` replays. A physics or hole change that breaks a line fails that test: run the solver again.

## Practice and the open world

- `/golf` is `GameHub` with Practice (`Practice.tsx`); `/golf/practice` (`GolfHub`) lists each course's holes with your best, the leader and who's on it, Play from the first hole you haven't finished, and the open world. Holes are `/golf/<course>/<n>` (`plains`, `cold`), each its own live world keyed by its id; GolfWorld is keyed by hole id.
- Bumper Plains: six short bumper holes plus Battle Grounds' three (minefields, speed-pad highways, ramps over the void). Cold Start: nine on ice (`theme: "ice"` gives `toy.ts` `ICE_PALETTE`, `surface: "packed"` slides, `ice` zones slide further, `snow` brakes).
- Items carry through a run: what you held when you last sank a hole goes on (`golfBags`, written by `leave`, read by `join` into the join command's `bag`); a retry goes back to `startBag`; leaving unfinished carries nothing; hole 1 starts empty-handed. Sinking shows `HoleResult` with the ball already back on the tee.
- Driftwood Bay (`sandbox.ts`, `/golf/world`, key `sandbox`): an island in the sea (coastline by Chaikin), a lagoon and bridge with a sliding crate, a ramp jump, a bumper park, a minefield with the best boxes, a speed-pad highway to an islet with its own flag, sand, portals, an ice rink, wind, a spinner, four flags and eight tees. Sinking puts you back on a tee and counts it; boxes return after 20 s, bumpers 25 s, map mines 15 s; every item works on everyone.

## Previews (the mode page and lobby)

`GolfHoles` (`HolesPreview.tsx`) under the mode page's settings and in the lobby (host under the seed, everyone else under the chips). A generated course always has a real seed there (rolled on first show, `rollFirstSeed`; the dice rolls another); it asks `golf:requestPreview` 300 ms after the hole-shaping settings settle (only course, holes, terrain, style, hazards, seed, boxes, so ticking items doesn't ask again), and `golf:makePreview` makes holes one at a time into `golfPreviews` keyed by `genKey`; `golf:preview` streams them in. A hand-made course shows at once. A game started on that seed uses them. Jobs quiet too long restart; previews untouched for two days are cleared through their job rows. Tap a hole for the big one in a `Sheet`.

## The screens

- **The look** (`toy.ts`, colors in `palette.ts`), in the direction of Putt Party and GamePigeon, with the app's design system around it and no Wordle tiles (results are a sheet with the score's name): flat, straight down, fixed natural golf colors (`PALETTE`: mowing stripes on the diagonal, cream rail, terracotta bumpers; `ICE_PALETTE` keeps the board mid-tone so a white ball stands out), on a dot grid in the theme's `--background`/`--border` (`refreshTheme`, `VOID_FALLBACK`). One color family per kind of thing; playthings stand out by brightness, not hue alone; no fake depth. Every part is its own function taking how far along its animation is (`drawBall`, `drawBumper`, `drawFlag`, `drawBox`, `drawCup`...), so card art and the game draw the same things. Life is in motion: the ball pops in, stretches at speed, squashes (in its own frame, so it never snaps) and spirals into the cup; bumpers swell; flags fly off (eased so a ball zipping past doesn't flicker them); boxes burst into their item's name; bangs shake the camera. Text is drawn once, large, into its own canvas then scaled, because Safari renders tiny fractional font sizes badly and changing sizes snap between hinted sizes. Effects colors are `FX`; the title over a hole and the Flash Camera's white are `OVERLAY`.
- **GolfWorld** (`FullscreenStage`): the canvas (`aria-label` "<course>, hole N of M" in a game, "<course>, hole N" on practice, "<course>, an open course"; `data-turn` mine/theirs in a game; the stage's `data-mode` is my ball's mode), the top bar, `TargetHint`, the round's banner (Catching up / Skip), the dock, the flash layer, the hole's title fading out, `HoleResult`, and "Teeing up" until the first frame. Input: drag back anywhere to aim (power `min(1, pull/maxPull)^1.35`), release to shoot; while rolling, Brake/Jump fire on the finger going down (a rolling ball won't wait for a tap to finish); a targeted item waits for a tap on the map; otherwise drag pans, two fingers pinch, the wheel zooms. Small holes show whole and stay still (`!box.big`); big ones follow the ball (leaning toward the cup while aiming, along its way while rolling), pause following 1.5 s after a pan, and "See the whole map" toggles the overview.
- **Layout and landscape**: `layout()` (a ResizeObserver) measures the open part between the bars into `Box` (`top`, `bottom`, `left`, `right`) and frames the camera in it (`ox` at the middle of the open width, `fit` the whole board with an 8 px `OPEN_GAP`, `play` = the short side / 24 units). In portrait the bar is on top (`top` below it). In landscape (`phone-landscape:`) `FloatingTopBar` becomes a rail down the left (`--rail-width` 13rem on the stage, pills `RAIL_PILL`; the practice bar keeps its pill under Back, the game's Menu sits at the foot), detected by the bar being taller than wide, so `left` is the rail's right edge and `top` is just the gap; the dock, `TargetHint`, the banner and the title sit to the right of the rail (`BESIDE_RAIL`, the dock's own classes); `HoleResult` centers at `max-w-md` and scrolls; the hole arrow pointing at an off-screen cup stays inside the open part (`inset` left/right). Safe areas only through `var(--safe-*)`.
- **Dock** (`Hud.tsx`): three item slots (56 px, `Use <item>` / `Empty slot`, the armed one filled), my ball's status ("Frozen", "Caffeinated"...), "Slow connection", "<item> next shot", then Brake/Jump while rolling, or the overview button (big maps) and "Start the hole again"/"Back to a tee" (not in a game).
- **A game** (`GolfGame.tsx`): `RoundBar` (Back, the Scorecard pill with whose turn it is via `useTurnText` or the live hole clock `Countdown`, my strokes, Menu); a ding when the turn comes to me; a sink toasts its score name; `HoleCard` (a `Sheet` over the next hole: scores best first, "Picked up", running totals against par, next hole) appears when a hole's scores land and goes after 9 s; `Building` while holes are made; `Finished`: the shared `Result`, the card and `SeedRow` (copy the seed, or "Play these holes" with `?settings=`). `GolfMenu`: the scorecard, the seed, the shared settings and rules, Resign.
- **Sound** (`audio.ts`): golf's own Web Audio (putt, wood knocks, rubber bumpers, a rolling hiss that follows speed and ground, cup, boxes, blasts, every item), soft envelopes, filtered noise, a small reverb, "playback" audio session like the app's default; every tap anywhere wakes the context with a silent buffer (iOS only starts audio from a whole tap). Only game events make sound.

- `SOLUTIONS["plains-1"][0]` is an ace from the main tee for a lone ball (`shoot(page, ...SOLUTIONS[...])`); with two balls on plains-1, `shoot(pa, -2.78, 0.84)` from the first tee.
- `testing:golfWorld({ key })` gives `{ awake, snapshotTick }`: wait for `awake === false` and `__golf.from() === snapshotTick` to know the server's settled and the phone has its snapshot (`worldAsleep`).

- Games go through `openLobby(pa, "golf", "turns", { Course: "Bumper Plains", Holes: "3" })` and `fillLobby` (the real start hook); `testing:startMatch` refuses golf. Whose turn: `getMatch(id).turn` against `__golf.me()`, and the canvas `data-turn`. The turn passing bumps `moveCount`.

- Landscape: `page.setViewportSize(LANDSCAPE)`, `simulateSafeAreas(page, SAFE_LANDSCAPE)`, `holdsStill`, `expectFitsScreen`; `rotate(page, ...)` mid-hole re-frames the camera.

## Gotchas

- Field names on `Sim`, `Ball`, `WorldState` and `Packed` are stored in snapshots and starts: renaming one breaks every stored world.
- Don't put simulation, or writes another player also makes, into `golf:act`.
- `GolfWorld`'s loop (`useGolfLoop`) runs once per mount (and again when the server clock becomes ready), reading its props through a latest ref captured at start; worlds are keyed by course id, so a new hole is a new mount.
- `__golf` and `golf-seen:` are what e2e and catching up lean on; keep their shapes.
- Touching `plains.ts`/`battle.ts`/`coldstart.ts`/`shapes.ts`/`engine.ts` can move a hole by a bit: run `npx vitest run convex/golf` (solutions replay) and, if a line breaks, `bun scripts/golf-solve.ts`.
- Touching `engine.ts`/`world.ts`/`dmath.ts` can change the pinned fingerprint: that's a physics change for every phone at once.
- `npx knip` fails on unused exports: drawing helpers in `toy.ts` stay module-private unless something imports them.
