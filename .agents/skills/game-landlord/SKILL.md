# Landlord

AGENTS.md is the overview and the `framework` skill covers matches, turns, secrets, pushes and the harness; the `ui` skill covers `FullscreenStage`, `FloatingTopBar`, sheets and landscape. This is what you need on top of them to change Landlord. The game is shown as "Monopoly" everywhere a player reads it; the code, paths and functions say `landlord`.

## Mode and settings

One async mode, `landlord:classic` ("Classic", title "Monopoly", 2 to 8, `minPlayers` 2 so the host taps Start), in `convex/landlord/modes.ts` (`LANDLORD_GAME`, `LANDLORD_MODES`). Endings: `richest` (short game or a round limit), `last_player`, and `resign` when one of two players resigns. Settings, turned into engine `Rules` by `rulesOf` in `convex/landlord.ts`: `cash` Starting cash ($1,000 to $3,000, default $1,500); `game` 0 Classic / 1 Short game (`Rules.short`); `rounds` No limit / 10 / 20 / 30; `auctions` On (official) / Off (a declined property stays with the bank); `pot` Free Parking jackpot Off / Taxes and fines / Plus $500 (seeded with $500 at the start and every time it's taken); `goDouble` landing on GO pays $400; `jailRent` collect rent in jail (official yes); `firstLap` 1 means nobody buys before passing GO once; `speed` the speed die (adds $1,000 to everyone's start, its official setup); `limited` the bank's 32 houses and 12 hotels, or unlimited. `rules.ts` (`landlordRules`) is How to play written from the settings.

## Naming

Every name a player reads (squares, cards, the game's title on screen) lives in `board.ts`, `cards.ts`, `modes.ts`, `art.tsx` (the banner's deeds) and `og/games/landlord.mjs`, so a rename touches only those, plus `engine.test.ts`, which pins the board's names in order. Numbers and effects are the classic US board's; wording is our own. The Mega edition isn't in: its title deeds couldn't be sourced reliably.

## The rules engine (`engine.ts`)

- A game is `{ s, decks, events }`: `s` (`MonoState`) is everything public and goes in `matches.state`; `decks` is the order of both decks, the game's secret; `events` is what the last action did, in order. `act(game, seat, action, rng)` clones, applies, runs, prunes stale offers and returns a new game, or throws a sentence a player can read ("It's not your roll.").
- **The step queue.** A turn is `s.todo`, a queue of `Step`s (`walk`, `land` with the dice a utility counts and a card's `mod` of doubled station rent or ten-times utility, `pay`, `mrm` (the speed die's Mr. Monopoly), `auction`, `after` (roll again on doubles, else next), `next` (the next live seat, bumping `round` when it wraps, ending at the round limit), `resume` (back to the roll the game was waiting on)). `run` executes steps while `phase` is `idle` (guarded at 500), taking `next` when the queue is empty, and stops at the first that needs someone.
- **Phases** (`s.phase.k`): `roll` (also the jail choices), `buy`, `auction` (`high`, `leader`, `in` = who's still bidding in turn order from whoever's turn it is), `debt` (`from`, `to`, `amount`, `why`), `bus` (the speed die's bus: move by either die or both), `pick` (triples: anywhere), `idle` (only ever transient: the sim test fails if a game is left idle), `over`.
- `actor(s)`: who the game waits on: the debtor, in an auction the next bidder after the leader (in turn order, wrapping), else whoever's turn it is; -1 when over.
- **Anytime actions.** `build`, `sell`, `sellAll`, `mortgage`, `unmortgage`, `offer`, `accept`, `reject`, `withdraw` and `resign` don't need the turn. Each has a `cant*` helper returning why not or null (`cantBuild`, `cantSell`, `cantMortgage`, `cantUnmortgage`, `cantTrade`, `cantAccept`); the screen disables buttons and shows the same sentence. Build and sell evenly (a hotel needs four houses on every street of the group, three in the short game, `hotelAt`); no building on a group with a mortgage; mortgaging needs the group clear of buildings. With limited buildings the bank's stock (`s.bank`) runs out; a hotel the bank hasn't the houses to break down can't be sold alone, only the whole group's buildings (`sellAll`, offered as "Sell group").
- **Money** moves only through `pay`, which emits a `pay` event carrying everyone's cash and the pot afterwards. Mortgage gives half the price; lifting costs that plus 10% rounded up ($75 comes to $83). `spendable` holds back a winning bid in a running auction. `worth` (cash, properties at price or half if mortgaged, buildings at cost) places the short game and the round limit.
- **Rent** (`rentOf`): nothing if unowned or mortgaged; streets double unbuilt with the whole group; stations $25 doubling per station owned (twice that from a card); utilities 4x or 10x the dice (10x a fresh roll from a card, logged as a `roll` with `util`). No rent to someone in jail when `jailRent` is off.
- **Jail.** Doubles out (and move by them), $50 (`payJail`), a card (`cardJail`, which goes back under its deck), or on the third miss pay and move anyway. Three doubles in a turn is jail. Going to jail ends doubles and, with the speed die, Mr. Monopoly.
- **Decks** are drawn from the top and put back underneath, never reshuffled; a get-out-of-jail card stays with its holder until used, traded or they go bankrupt (then it goes back under its deck, or to the creditor).
- **Debts.** A `pay` step the payer can't cover becomes the `debt` phase. The debtor raises money any way they like; the moment their cash covers it, `act` pays it and play goes on. `pay` (the action) is only for when they already have it. `bankrupt` is refused while selling and mortgaging everything (`raisable`) could still cover it.
- **Bankruptcy** (`bankrupt`): buildings go back to the bank at half; to a player, everything goes to them (they pay 10% interest on the mortgaged ones as a queued `pay`); to the bank (or the jackpot), properties return unmortgaged and are auctioned one by one (`interject` puts the auctions in now mid-turn, before the next roll between turns, or after the pending decision). Their pending payments and offers go; an auction they were in or leading carries on without them; their turn moves on. The game is over when one is left, or at the second bankruptcy in the short game. Resigning is going bankrupt to whoever you owe, else the bank.
- **Auctions** are open: anyone still in may bid any whole amount above the high bid (up to their cash; the dock offers +$10 and a sheet); dropping out is for good; the leader can't drop out. It ends when everyone but the leader has dropped out (sold) or everyone has with no bid (unsold).
- **Trades** (`offer`): cash, properties and get-out-of-jail cards each way; nothing in a group with buildings. Accepting re-checks everything (`cantAccept`): both sides must still be able to pay the 10% on mortgaged properties they get. Each side may lift the mortgages they get at once (`lift`: the mortgage plus that one 10%) instead of paying 10% now and 10% again later. `prune` drops offers that can't happen any more after every action (a property changed hands, someone went out), but not ones merely short of cash, which can still be raised.
- **The speed die** (from your second lap, `lapped`): 1-3 add on; the bus (6) is a choice of either die or both (utilities count both white dice); Mr. Monopoly (4, 5) goes on after landing to the next property nobody owns, or with none left the next one where you owe rent; triples go anywhere (`pick`) and end the turn there.
- **The short game**: `newGame` deals everyone two shuffled deeds, paid for at their price; hotels after three houses; over at the second bankruptcy, richest wins.
- Rngs: `newGame` and `act` take one (default `Math.random`); tests pass scripted dice or `mulberry(seed)`.

## Server (`convex/landlord.ts`)

- `start`: `newGame` with `rulesOf`, then `commit` with `first` (the "Monopoly is on" opening pushes through `startPushes`: "You roll first." or "Chris rolls first.").
- `commit` saves a move: the decks secret, a `landlordLog` row (`seq` = the first event's number, `by` = the mover's seat or -1 for the game itself, `events`, the public `state` after, `at`), then either `finishBySeats(standings)` or `passTurn` to `actor`'s player with the reminder as its timer, then `tell`.
- No time limit: `timeout` does nothing and `timeoutWarning` is empty. Instead `remind` (the `passTurn` timer, `REMIND_MS` 4 hours, then every 12 hours after 4 nudges, `EAGER_REMINDERS`) pushes "Still your move" to whoever it's waiting on (everyone still bidding but the leader in an auction), and fizzles once anyone moves (`moveCount`).
- `tell`: one push per player, the most pressing (lowest rank): 0 a decision now theirs (only when `fresh`: "You owe $X", "Auction: Boardwalk", or "Your turn" with the last two things that happened as the body), 1 an offer to them, 2 an auction or a bid, 3 their trade taken or turned down, 4 rent paid to them, 5 someone bankrupt. Never the mover, never anyone out.
- `move { id, action }` (`seated`, any time). Resign goes through the shared `matches.resign` and the `resign` hook (the client's `useLandlordMove` excludes `resign`).
- `log { id, from? }`: the state as of the last move finished by `from` (an event number), plus every move after it. With no `from`, everything since the signed-in player's own last move (spectators get the whole game). Null for a game with no log rows; the screen then just shows the state.

## The log and the show (`show.ts`)

The screen draws a show, not the state: everything since this phone last looked plays out in order (dice, the piece hopping square by square, money flying off the cash chips, the card drawn, auctions, buildings), then it's live, and only then can you act. Each event carries what it changed outright (everyone's cash after a payment, the new owner), so `apply` plays them back without the rules.
- `ShowRunner` is an external store (`useSyncExternalStore`): the screen's effects feed it inputs (the real state, my seat, names, the camera) and log pages; it plays and patches its snapshot. It stops playing when the screen unmounts.
- Where it starts: `landlord-seen:<match>` in localStorage (the last event number this phone showed), or, never having looked, wherever the server starts it (my own last move), pinned in `from` once said since that moves with every move I make.
- It writes `landlord-seen` at the end of each move it plays and when it settles. `settle` shows the real state once nothing's queued and the log has reached it; if the real state lands before the log row that explains it, the screen stays busy (`behind`).
- Pace: holds per event (850ms a roll, 2.4s a card, 150ms a hop...), x0.65 while catching up, x0.6 past 20 queued and x0.35 past 60. Skip, the page hidden, or a crash in one event jumps to the end (a bad event never wedges the screen: the rest are dropped and it settles on the real state).
- Catching up (the pill's "Catching up" chip and Skip to now in the dock) when more than 3 of someone else's events are waiting on opening, or over 120 pile up while watching.
- Sounds (`sfx`, `live` for what's happening now): tick per hop, land, invalid (jail), shuffle (dice), flip (card), chips (rent, buy, auction, card, tax), payout (jackpot), turn (my turn, live only), place (build), knock (auction), slam (sold), lose (bankrupt); win or lose at the end, once the show's done.
- The feed (newest last, 80 kept) is the menu's "What happened". `line` is the pill's text while busy.

## The screen

- Fullscreen (`fullscreen` in `GAME_UI` for active and finished games): the dotted stage, the board under the camera, the dice and card floating in the middle of the open part (`Overlays`), the floating bar (Back, the pill edged in the colour of whoever the game's waiting on with their avatar and the status, Whole board / Zoom in, Menu), and the dock.
- The board (`Board.tsx`) is drawn big (`SIDE` 916 board pixels: corners `C` 116, sides `W` 76) so names read at `CLOSE` (1.05). Square 0 (GO) is bottom-right, play runs clockwise. Each square is a `Button` (`data-pos`, `data-owner`, named "Boardwalk, owned by Chris, a hotel"); owners show as a seat-coloured edge and face on the outer side; houses and hotels sit on the colour band; mortgaged squares are struck through. The middle has the MONOPOLY tiles, the jackpot, the bank's houses and hotels, and a speed-die note. Pieces are avatars on the squares' outer edge (`spotOf`), spread along it when sharing, inside the jail cell when jailed, the current player's a little bigger (`data-token`, `data-pos`).
- The dock (`Dock.tsx`, `data-dock`, `data-busy` while the show plays): everyone's cash chips in turn order (tap one: their `PlayerSheet` and the camera on them; money floats off them), then your move (Roll / Roll again; in jail Use card, Pay $50, Roll; Buy for $X or Auction it / Pass, or Raise $X to buy; an auction's Drop out, Bid… and Bid $high+10; the bus's three moves; triples' "tap any square"; a debt's Raise $X, Pay, or Bankrupt behind a confirm), then Properties and Trade (with a badge counting offers to you). While busy a 60px spacer holds its height; finished, the shared `Result`.
- Sheets: `DeedSheet` (price, mortgage, house cost, owner, the rent table with today's row lit, and on your own build, sell, mortgage (always confirmed, `ConfirmMortgage`) or lift, each with its `cant*` reason; on someone else's "Ask Chris for it" opens a trade asking for it), `HoldingsSheet` (cash, what you could raise, your properties by group with sell / build / mortgage / lift), `TradeSheet`, `PlayerSheet`, the pick sheet ("Go to Boardwalk?"). The menu (`LandlordMenu`): standings by worth with cash, jail and swatches of what they own, What happened, then the shared settings, rules, share, and Resign.
- Landscape (`usePhoneLandscape` from `src/components/game/landscape.ts`): the board's viewport is inset by the side safe areas, and a side column on the right (`w-[min(22rem,48vw)]`, clear of the safe areas) holds the bar's buttons as a row over the dock as a panel (cash chips wrap; your move and the tools sit at its bottom, by the thumb; it scrolls if the result is long). Portrait is unchanged. On rotation the view is re-measured and the camera re-placed on whoever's up (the whole board if the game's over).

## The camera (`camera.ts`)

Motion values `x`, `y`, `k` that the board's transform reads directly (nothing re-renders as it moves). `useCamera(viewportRef, view)` takes the measured `CameraView` (`w`, `h`, and how much the bars cover on each side: `top`, `bottom`, `left`, `right`); the open part is what's left, and every bit of maths uses it: `fit` (the whole board at 96% of the open part), clamping (centred when smaller than the open part, otherwise no more than 40% of the open part past any edge, so an edge square can be centred), `focus` (a board point to the middle of the open part), `whole`. `LandlordGame` measures it (`measureView`): portrait, the floating bar covers the top and the dock the bottom (8px gap); landscape, the side column covers the right, and its top and bottom insets are the open part's. Before the first measurement it uses `UNMEASURED_VIEW`, and the first placement waits for a real one.
- The game points it at what's happening (`onLook` from the show: `fresh` hands the camera back with `release`). A finger drag (past 8px), a pinch, the wheel, a double tap (300ms, 30px: whole board, or close up where it landed) or the Whole board button (`hold`) take it, and it stays put until the game next has something new to show. A drag that ends on a square isn't a tap on it (`onClickCapture`). `overview` (at fit) flips the button between Whole board and Zoom in. A finished game shows the whole board.

## Bots (tests only)

`bot.ts` plays like a casual human: buys with a $150 cushion, bids up to a rough value, builds with spare cash, raises money by selling then mortgaging, answers every offer, and now and then offers cash for the last street of a group. Never seated in a real game. `nextBotSeat(s, isHuman)` is whoever a bot should act for next: someone a non-human offer waits on, else `actor`, or null if that's the human.

## Driving it in tests

- Unit: `engine.test.ts` builds games with `newGame(n, rules)` and scripted dice (`dice(1, 2, ...)`: each die is one rng call, so a roll is two values, three with the speed die). `sim.test.ts` has bots (`botMove`, `spare`, seeded `mulberry`) play 60 games for each of 8 rule mixes and checks after every action: no negative cash, every cash change explained by a `pay` event, buildings conserved, even building, no buildings on mortgaged groups, decks whole, never idle, never waiting on someone out, no unpaid debt that could be paid, no runaway steps, more than half finishing under the action cap. It must pass with the engine unchanged. `server.test.ts`: `game(t, n, settings)` queues players and starts it; pushes are pending `push:sendToUser` jobs. It covers the opening (everyone on GO, the decks only in the secret, the first player pushed), only the player on turn rolling, the log from `from` and from my own last move, the reminders fizzling, auction and bid pushes, trade pushes both ways, resigning in two-player ending it, and bots playing a whole game through the server to `richest` with a gapless log.
- `testing:landlordBots({ id, human?, steps? })` plays bots on the server for every seat but `human` until it's the human's decision (or `steps`); `testing:landlordPatch({ id, state })` merges fields into the state (give someone properties: `props`; move pieces or cash: `players`).
- e2e: start through `openLobby(page, "landlord", "classic", { "Starting cash": "$2,000" })` and `fillLobby`. Wait for the show with `[data-busy]` count 0 (`settledOn`); the dock is `[data-dock]`; squares `button[data-pos="N"]` or by name (`/^Mediterranean Avenue/`); pieces `[data-token="seat"]` with `data-pos`; cash chips `[data-seat="i"]`; "Whole board" before tapping a square that might be off screen. `window.__landlord` has `view()`, `real()`, `queued()`, `running()`, `playedTo()` for diagnosing a stuck show.
- `SCREENS=1 npm run test:e2e -- landlord` shoots `e2e/.state/screens/landlord-*.png` (mode, start, rolled, deed, catching-up, trade, midgame, overview, holdings, menu, overview-dark, landscape, landscape-whole). `SOAK=150` plays a phone against two bots for that many turns (`SOAK_SHORT=1` for the short game) and fails on a stuck show, a page error or the phone disagreeing with the server; then the bots finish and the phone skips to the result.

## Gotchas

- Stored games carry `MonoState` as is: never rename its fields or `Ev` fields, and keep `kind: "monopoly"`. Old phones replay old log rows.
- Every phone replays events without the rules, so an event must carry what it changed; a new kind of change needs an event (and a case in `apply`) or phones drift until they settle.
- `act` never mutates its input (it clones), so the show and the sim can keep the state before.
- The tie between a debt and raising money is automatic: never add a step that leaves a payable debt in place.
- Hidden information is only the deck order (the secret); everything else in `matches.state` is public on purpose.
- The show and the server can disagree for a moment by design (the state lands before its log row); the dock waits on `busy`, which includes `behind`.
- The dock's Bid sheet clamps to your cash; a bid over your cash or not above the high bid is refused by the engine anyway.
