# PvP Wordle

AGENTS.md is the overview and the `framework` skill covers matches, secrets, pushes and the harness; the `ui` skill covers `GameFrame`, sheets and landscape. One async mode, `wordle:classic` (2 to 8, `minPlayers` 2 so the host taps Start). Nothing in it may favour whoever opens the app first: everyone plays the same word at once, shopping order goes by coins (never by who shopped first), nobody sees who bought what or at whom until the word closes, and nobody loses a shop to a claim they couldn't see.

## Settings

In `convex/wordle/modes.ts`, read by `rulesOf` in `convex/wordle.ts`: `goal` Words to win (3, 5, 7; default 5), `wordTime` Time per word (no limit, 12h, 1 day, 2 days; default 1 day), `shop` (on/off). A game has at most `roundsFor(goal)` = goal + 3 words. Endings: `words` (someone reached the goal), `rounds` (the words ran out), `resign` / `last_player`.

## File map

- `convex/wordle.ts`: `guess` (`{ id, word, round, row }`, `row` = the rows the phone saw, a stale one is refused), `shop` (`{ id, round, cart: { item, target }[] }`, an empty cart passes, changeable until the shop closes), `pick` (the old one-item form, kept for phones on an old build: a cart of one), `play` (`{ id, round, item, rush? }`, a card from your hand or bought on the spot), `typing` (`{ id, letters }`, a draft count others see as dots), `board` (my view), internal `deadline` and `remind`, `wordleHooks`, and `guessAs` / `pickAs` / `rulesOf` / `REMIND_MS` for tests. Never rename these.
- `convex/wordle/engine.ts`: pure rules. `score`, `isGuessable`, `newGame`, `guess`, `play`, `cantPlay`, `rushPrice`, `choose`, `reconcile`, `leftForSeat`, `cantGuess`, `cantPick`, `pickStock`, `payFor`, `waitingOn`, `roundDone`, `lastWord`, `buyOrder`, `finishRound`, `leave`, `standings`, `fogged`, `jammed`, `keyColors`, `ITEMS`.
- `convex/wordle/words.ts` (`GUESSES`, every 5-letter ENABLE word, concatenated) and `convex/wordle/answers.ts` (`ANSWERS`, 2000 common ones, server only). Both generated by `python3 scripts/gen-wordle-words.py`: ENABLE (public domain) words, answers being those in SCOWL's common lists (size 35 and under) minus plurals, past tenses and a hand-kept list of rude words and names (`LEFT_OUT`), ranked by Peter Norvig's web word counts (`count_1w.txt`). Only ENABLE words are shipped. No comments allowed in them, so the sources live here.
- `convex/wordle/text.ts`: `buyLine` (log and push lines), `roundPush`.
- `convex/wordle/preview.ts`: the link preview. Only public numbers, plus the last closed word's colors (the word itself once the game's over).
- `src/games/wordle/`: `WordleGame.tsx` (screen, `usePlay`, `useLive`, done panel, incoming banner, players strip, log and player sheets), `Board.tsx` (the grid: measured tile size, flips, shake, hop on a solve, the fog overlay), `Keyboard.tsx`, `ShopSheet.tsx`, `parts.tsx` (`ItemIcon`, `Coins`, `MiniBoard`, mark colours), `art.tsx`, `rules.ts`, `summary.ts`.
- `og/games/wordle.mjs` (colours under `WORDLE` in `og/palette.mjs`).

## Rules and invariants

- A round is one word, the same for everyone (`secrets` with no user holds `{ words }`, one per possible round). Each player gets `max` rows (6, minus Short fuse, plus Extra row, kept within 3..8).
- Pay when your word ends (`payFor`): `BASE_PAY` + 1 per position you turned green + (if solved) `SOLVE_PAY` + rows left, doubled per Payday on you, then halved (rounded down) per Tax. Start with `START_COINS`. `guesses` adds rows used, or `ROWS + 1` for a miss. A solve is worth about 11, a miss 2 to 5: prices are set against that, so a good word buys one mid item or two cheap ones, and the strong ones take saving.
- The round waits on every live player to finish their word and then shop (a cart, or pass). No shop on the last word (`lastWord`: the final round, or someone done has reached the goal), so then it waits on words only. `waitingOn` drives `matches.waitingOn`.
- Stock (`pickStock`): every item gets a random 0..cap each word (cap `ceil(alive / 2)`, `ceil(alive / 3)` for `rare` ones), topped up so at least `IN_STOCK_AT_LEAST` items have some. Items at 0 show blacked out. Old games' stock lists only some items: a missing one is 0.
- Shopping order (`buyOrder`): fewest coins going into the word (`coins - earned`, fixed for the whole word), then fewest solved going in, then seat. A cart (up to `MAX_CART`, all of it affordable at once) lives in `Mine.cart` (old games may have only `Mine.pick`; `cartOf` reads both).
- Claims: what's left for you (`leftForSeat`, the `board` query's `left`) is the stock minus what everyone ahead of you has in their carts; carts behind you don't count. After every cart change or resign, `reconcile` walks the order and trims any cart that no longer fits (a poorer player took the last one, its target left, or it costs more than they have, e.g. a pick saved before a price rise): those entries go to `Mine.lost`, the player's `picked` goes false so the word waits on them again, and once the match is saved they get a "Shop again" push (none if the shop is closing). An open shop sheet trims its draft the same way (`fitCart`). The counts aren't anonymous in every case: at two players, a drop in what's left for you can only be your opponent's claim, aimed at you if it's an attack. So every closed shop sells everything in every cart; `got: false` only happens in a game from before this rule.
- Closing (`finishRound`): anyone not done is closed as a miss (the deadline passes `force`); each player in `buyOrder` buys their whole cart in order. Shields are counted first (one block each), then each attack in buy order: a shielded target bounces it. Pickpocket takes up to `STEAL` of what's left after everyone's bought; everything else becomes an `Effect` on the target's next word. Jam picks 2 letters from `earotlisnc`, locked for the first `JAM_TURNS` guesses (the server refuses them). Fog hides rows 0..1 (`FOG_ROWS`) once you've guessed past them (the `board` query drops their letters and colours); kept small and pricey on purpose, since people leave a word half-done and come back to it. Blackout leaves the keyboard uncoloured (and hides a Clue). Tax and Payday change the next word's pay. Hint writes one letter and its place into that player's secret; Clue writes one other letter (`Mine.clue`), shown yellow on their keyboard.
- Items and prices (`ITEMS`): against them Blackout 6, Pickpocket 5, Jam 8, Tax 9, Fog 13, Short fuse 14 (rare); cards Clue 6, Shield 7, Payday 8, Extra row 10, Hint 12 (rare).
- Cards: everything for yourself goes into your hand (`Mine.hand`, secret, `MAX_HAND`, carried to the next word's secret by `finishRound`) instead of acting on the next word. `play` (`cantPlay`) plays one during a word you're still on: Extra row raises `max` now, Hint adds a green letter not already known (`Mine.hints`), Clue a letter in the word not already known (`Mine.clues`, shown yellow on the keyboard and as "In it:" in the hand row), Payday adds a `payday` effect for this word. Shield isn't played: it sits in the hand and the first attack at you in a shop uses it up. `rush` buys a Hint, Clue or Extra row on the spot for `rushPrice` (price × `RUSH`, rounded up), from no stock. Shopping order uses `Player.startCoins` (coins as the word opened), so mid-word spending and pickpockets don't move it. Pickpocket is an effect: when that player's next word pays, up to `STEAL` of it moves to the thrower (`Effect.took`).
- The closed word goes into `state.history` with everyone's guesses, marks and buys, so letters become public only once a word closes. While it's open, a player's guesses live only in their secret (`Mine`), and others' colours come back from `board` only once you're done with the word yourself.
- Winning: after a close, anyone on the goal ends it; standings by solved, then fewer guesses, then more coins; equal on all three share a place. Leaving makes you `out` (done and picked), last in standings; under two left ends it.

## Framework: `waitingOn`

`matches.waitingOn` (optional) lists everyone a simultaneous game waits on, alongside `turn`. `awaits(match, userId)` in `convex/lib/seats.ts` is what `badgeCount`, `attention` and `inbox` use, and `TurnChip` shows "Your move" or "Waiting on N". Wordle leaves `turn` unset, so the shared 8-hour `remind` never fires for it: `wordle:remind` pushes everyone still owed every 6 hours twice, then every 12, until the deadline. `finish` clears `waitingOn`.

## Pushes

Start: "PvP Wordle is on". A close: each live player gets "Word N is up" with what hit them (or bounced off their shield), or their tally. A cart trimmed by a poorer player's claim gets "Shop again". When everyone but one is in (3+ players), that one gets "Everyone's waiting on you". Results are the shared ones.

## The screen

`GameFrame`, not fullscreen. Portrait: top line (Word N / "It was CRANE" at the end, first to N, coins, Log), the players strip (`data-seat`, `data-solved`, `data-rows`, `data-done`; row dots, typing dots, effects on them), the board (`role=grid` "Your board", `data-rows`, `data-played`; `data-fog` on the fog overlay), then the dock at a fixed 184px: the keyboard (`role=group` "Keyboard", keys `data-key`, `data-mark`, `data-jammed`; each key's 44px hit area is an `::after` behind every key, in an `isolate` row) or the done panel (`data-my-word` won/lost, coins earned, everyone's colours as `MiniBoard`s `[data-board-of] [data-marks]`, the Shop button naming your cart, or "Shop again" after a claim took something from it, who it's waiting on). The shop sheet pins your coins after the cart, your shopping place, the cart as removable chips and Pass / Buy under its title (`[data-cart]`, `[data-lost]`); below, "Throw at someone" and "For you", each item a button named `"<Name>, <price> coins, <n> left"` (`data-item`, `data-left` = left for you), tapping adds one (with several opponents it opens a "Throw <Name> at" group first). Landscape: top line, players and the keyboard (or done panel) on the left, the board as `aside`. The incoming banner (`data-incoming`) drops over the board at the start of a word you were hit in. Physical keyboards work too. Sounds: pick/recall typing, flip per tile, bingo on a solve, sweep on a miss, invalid on a refused word, tick/knock/ghost when others guess or finish, turn on a new word, slam when hit, chips on buying, win/lose.

## Driving it in tests

- Unit: `engine.test.ts` builds tables with `newGame` and a fixed rng and sets `stock` to all items; `server.test.ts` goes through `matches.queue`/`startGame` and uses `testing:wordleWord` for the answer.
- `testing:wordleWord({ id })` (current answer), `wordleGuessAs({ id, userId, word })`, `wordlePickAs({ id, userId, item, target?, more? })` (`more` adds cart entries), `wordlePatch({ id, players?, stock? })` (merge per-seat fields, e.g. `{ coins, earned: 0, startCoins }` to set the shopping order; replace the stock).
- e2e (`e2e/wordle.spec.ts`): `openLobby(page, "wordle", "classic", settings)` and `fillLobby`; type through the Keyboard group; poll `getMatch` for `rows`, `picked`, `round`. `SCREENS=1 npm run test:e2e -- wordle` writes `e2e/.state/screens/wordle-*.png`.

## Gotchas

- `WordleState`, `Player`, `Mine`, `Buy` and `Round` are stored: never rename their fields; new ones optional.
- The answer lists are indexed by position (5 letters each); regenerating them changes nothing for running games, whose words are already in their secret.
- `typing` writes a draft at most once per change in letter count; drafts are cleared when a guess lands and when a word closes.
